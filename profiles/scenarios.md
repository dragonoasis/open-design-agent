# Scenario Profiles

Use these as risk overlays. They do not prescribe a visual style.

## Marketing and launch

- Prove the proposition with authentic evidence.
- Sequence claim, mechanism, proof, objection, and action.
- Keep the first viewport focused.
- Ensure reduced-motion and low-power paths retain the argument.

## Commerce and checkout

- Support comparison, availability, total cost, delivery, returns, and trust.
- Preserve cart and form state.
- Distinguish brand color from success, warning, and error.
- Make destructive and irreversible effects explicit.

## Dashboard and analytics

- Begin with decisions and monitoring needs, not chart variety.
- Choose visualization by analytical task.
- Preserve exact values, units, freshness, filters, and comparison context.
- Design loading, no-data, partial-data, stale, and permission states.

## Editors and creation tools

- Protect user work with autosave, history, conflict, and recovery behavior.
- Keep selection, focus, mode, and scope visible.
- Support keyboard efficiency without hiding discoverability.
- Treat canvas, inspector, and output as coordinated regions.

## Documentation and knowledge

- Optimize findability, scan, reading, copy, citation, version, and return.
- Separate navigation, prose, examples, and API truth.
- Keep code, tables, diagrams, and long words responsive.
- Surface version and deprecation context.

## Forms and workflows

- Ask only for needed information.
- Group by user intent, not backend schema.
- Specify validation timing, dependency, persistence, and recovery.
- Keep errors connected to fields and summaries where needed.

## Search and discovery

- Design query entry, suggestions, refinement, results, ranking explanation, empty, and recovery together.
- Preserve query and filter state.
- Distinguish no match, unavailable data, and system failure.

## AI and chat

- Show model/system status, streaming, interruption, retry, edit, and provenance.
- Let the user scroll away without forced anchoring; provide jump-to-latest.
- Separate user, assistant, tool, system, attachment, and error events.
- Mark uncertainty and generated content; do not imply human authorship.

## Onboarding

- Move toward first value through a real task.
- Request permissions in context.
- Make optional education skippable and replayable.
- Measure activation without blocking experienced users.

## Mobile product

- Choose a coherent platform mode.
- Design safe areas, system bars, virtual keyboard, back, interruption, and restoration.
- Keep navigation and target reach plausible on real devices.
- Avoid shrinking a desktop website into a phone frame.

## International and multilingual

- Test expansion, truncation, wrapping, bidirectionality, mixed scripts, names, addresses, dates, numbers, and pluralization.
- Mirror direction-sensitive layout and icons deliberately.
- Do not mirror media or meaning blindly.

## High-risk domains

For health, finance, safety, identity, privacy, children, or legal workflows:

- identify applicable regulation and qualified review
- raise evidence and confirmation requirements
- minimize dark patterns and coercion
- design correction, audit, consent, and support paths
- never let visual confidence imply unverified accuracy
