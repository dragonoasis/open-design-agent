# Surface Profiles

Select per surface, not per company or product.

## Persuade

**Success:** a visitor understands the proposition, trusts the evidence, and takes a deliberate next step.

Prioritize:

- a clear claim and evidence sequence
- authentic product, service, customer, data, or craft proof
- narrative pacing and purposeful visual identity
- one unmistakable primary conversion path
- objection handling and trust cues

Allow:

- stronger display typography, imagery, variance, and motion
- chapter-like surface changes
- campaign-specific art direction

Reject:

- fabricated proof
- generic benefit-card repetition
- spectacle that hides the offer or action
- campaign styling in checkout or frequent-use controls

## Operate

**Success:** a person completes repeated tasks accurately, efficiently, and recoverably.

Prioritize:

- state clarity and predictable behavior
- information density matched to expertise
- keyboard and input efficiency
- progressive disclosure of complexity
- error prevention, recovery, and auditability
- stable location and low interaction cost

Allow:

- dense tables, command interfaces, inspectors, and shortcuts when the audience benefits
- restrained brand expression in chrome and data

Reject:

- motion that delays work
- oversized campaign typography
- hidden controls and decorative ambiguity
- carding every data group

## Read

**Success:** a person can find, understand, remember, cite, and return to content.

Prioritize:

- editorial hierarchy and information scent
- readable typography and stable measure
- navigation, contents, anchors, metadata, and related context
- media captions, provenance, and alternatives
- long-content performance and return position

Allow:

- expressive typography and imagery when comprehension remains primary
- section-specific visual registers inside a stable shell

Reject:

- scroll effects that interrupt selection or reading
- low-contrast “quiet” text
- decorative cards that fragment continuous thought

## Experience

**Success:** exploration, presence, play, emotion, or demonstration is the product outcome.

Prioritize:

- a legible interaction model
- orientation, agency, progress, and escape
- a meaningful relation between medium and subject
- equivalent access to essential content
- performance tiers and reduced-effects paths

Allow:

- spatial navigation, cinematic sequencing, rich sound, and high motion when they are integral
- deliberate novelty with onboarding proportional to novelty

Reject:

- spectacle with no subject-specific purpose
- inaccessible essential content hidden in canvas or gesture
- Experience conventions leaking into routine product work

## Mixed surfaces

Name the dominant mode of each region. For example:

- marketing page: Persuade
- pricing comparison: Persuade + Operate
- checkout: Operate
- documentation: Read + Operate
- interactive product demo: Experience + Persuade

When modes conflict, separate surfaces or establish a clear local priority.
