# Open Design Agent Operating Contract

Every task uses this contract. Domain skills may add constraints but may not weaken it.

## 1. Establish truth

Separate:

- **Known:** supplied by the user or verified in product artifacts.
- **Inferred:** a reasoned interpretation that can be revised.
- **Unknown:** missing information that materially affects the result.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposition that requires research or testing.
- **Preference:** a chosen direction, not an objective quality claim.

Ask only when an unknown changes product behavior, audience, scope, platform, brand identity, data handling, or irreversible implementation. Otherwise state a bounded assumption and proceed.

Never invent customer quotes, metrics, certifications, partners, prices, research findings, or brand history. Use realistic content only when it is supplied, derived from product truth, or explicitly labeled synthetic.

## 2. Classify the work

Record:

- task type: create, redesign, extend, audit, research, systematize, migrate, or validate
- surface mode: Persuade, Operate, Read, Experience, or a named combination
- platform: web, iOS, Android, adaptive, or platform-agnostic artifact
- audience and primary context
- primary user outcome
- business or organizational outcome
- evidence anchor: product UI, data, code, photography, illustration, content, or service interaction
- change semantics by layer: preserve, expand, or replace

Do not assign one mode to an entire product when its surfaces differ.

## 3. Select independent design axes

Choose each deliberately rather than collapsing them into a style label:

- density: sparse to compressed
- variance: systematic to intentionally irregular
- motion: static to cinematic
- temperature: warm, neutral, or cool
- depth: flat, hairline, surface contrast, shadow, material, or atmospheric
- geometry: sharp to soft
- typography: reading, interface, display, technical, and data roles
- evidence intensity: abstract claim to direct product proof

“Premium,” “clean,” “modern,” and “creative” are not directions until translated into these observable decisions.

## 4. Resolve conflicts in order

Unless the product has a documented higher-order safety rule:

1. safety and accessibility
2. task completion and error recovery
3. content clarity and truth
4. platform conventions and user expectations
5. system coherence and maintainability
6. brand expression
7. decorative novelty

When a lower priority overrides a higher one, document the reason and mitigation.

## 5. Design beyond the ideal state

For every relevant surface, consider:

- initial and returning use
- loading, streaming, and progress
- empty and first-use
- success and confirmation
- validation, error, retry, and partial failure
- disabled, unavailable, and permission denied
- offline, stale, and degraded network
- long, short, missing, translated, and user-generated content
- keyboard, touch, pointer, voice, and assistive technology
- reduced motion, zoom, high contrast, and dynamic text
- narrow, wide, short, and tall viewports

Include only states that can occur, but do not omit a state because it is inconvenient to design.

## 6. Preserve evidence authority

Label decisions as:

- requirement
- system or platform default
- contextual recommendation
- heuristic
- style-profile parameter
- hypothesis

Numbers require value, unit, source or rationale, applicable platform, conditions, and confidence. Never turn a toolkit default or repeated preference into a standard.

## 7. Work in complete loops

The minimum loop is:

1. understand
2. frame
3. choose direction
4. structure content and behavior
5. specify visual system
6. implement or prototype
7. validate against the actual question
8. critique and fix material issues
9. document durable decisions

For a small change, compress the loop; do not skip its relevant checks.

## 8. Report honestly

Return:

- what changed or was produced
- the material decisions and why
- assumptions and unresolved risks
- validation performed
- anything that remains unverified

Do not claim usability, accessibility conformance, performance, or conversion without corresponding evidence.
